LearnGreek Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

LearnGreek is a Modern Greek vocabulary app. It is built to work entirely on your device, and it does not collect any information about you.

Data we collect

None. LearnGreek has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet and has no servers of its own, so nothing is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences locally using standard iOS storage: your favorite words, your saved sentences, your chosen base language, and your light/dark mode setting. This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app.

Speech

When you tap a word, the app uses Apple's built-in text-to-speech voice for Greek (el-GR) on your device. The app does not record audio and does not request microphone access.

Children

Because LearnGreek collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
